# News Papers 0.5.5

News Papers 0.5.5 makes Android shapes follow your pen and fixes phone sync that sent only a few changes at a time.

- **Shapes follow the pen.** In Auto mode the stroke is drawn exactly as you move the pen; only when you lift it does it snap to the closest shape. If the drawing is not clearly a shape, it stays as you drew it instead of becoming a straight line. Auto now recognizes lines, polylines, arrows, double arrows, triangles, rectangles and squares (also rotated), diamonds, other quadrilaterals, pentagons, hexagons, stars, other polygons, and circles and ellipses (also rotated), tolerating wobbly edges and rounded corners. Near-horizontal lines, near-squares, near-circles and near-equilateral or right triangles are straightened. The shape menu adds Double arrow, Triangle, Diamond, Pentagon, Hexagon and Star.
- **Phone sync sends everything at once.** Opening a paper several times used to queue a separate change each time, and each sync sent only one change per paper, so the pending count under Sync dropped by a few per tap. Changes to the same paper, folder or answer are now combined, and one sync sends the whole queue. Changes already waiting on your phone go out on the first sync after this update.
